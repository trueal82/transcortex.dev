The n8n stack

Your processes. Your hosting choice.

n8n connects your applications and coordinates recurring workflows. It can run on your own infrastructure or as SaaS with n8n Cloud. We bring together the right components: workflows, data storage, operations, and a language model where needed.

Compare hosting options

What makes up the stack

n8n coordinates the work

Triggers start the workflow. Interfaces retrieve and transfer data. Rules determine what happens next and how exceptions are handled.

Data storage & operations

The database, credentials, backups, and monitoring form part of the operating plan. Together, we define which execution data is needed and how long it should be retained.

AI where useful

An LLM handles suitable language and document tasks. The model can run locally or connect through a service. Many workflows need no LLM at all.

Your infrastructure or SaaS?

Either approach can make sense. The decision depends on your data requirements, access to your systems, and the resources available to run the platform.

Self-hosting

In your own environment

n8n runs on your servers or in a hosting environment you choose. You determine the infrastructure and arrange its operation.

  • Suited to internal systems and specific data-flow requirements.
  • Updates, backups, monitoring, and recovery need to be organised.
  • A local language model is an option when its quality and computing requirements fit the task.

A good fit when you want control over the environment and can arrange reliable operation.

SaaS · n8n Cloud

As a hosted service

n8n is used as a service; the provider hosts the platform. Your workflows and application connections are configured within it.

  • Get started without running your own n8n servers.
  • The plan, available features, and usage limits need to suit the project.
  • Access to internal systems and connections to AI services are planned separately.

A good fit when you want to reduce infrastructure work and the service meets your requirements.

How independent can the stack be?

A fully self-hosted stack is possible: n8n, data storage, and a local language model can run in your own environment. An isolated deployment also requires preparation of the necessary models, components, and maintenance arrangements.

Self-hosting alone does not mean all data stays internal. A workflow may call external email, ERP, or AI services. We therefore examine each data flow and review outbound connections, including telemetry and the requirements of the selected edition. Infrastructure and AI model selection are separate decisions.

We clarify the features, licensing conditions, and integrations your project needs. Bring your requirements to us for help with selection and implementation.
