n8n consulting and implementation for SMEs

We start with one clearly defined process. You get a practical assessment, an agreed implementation, and a documented handover. Transcortex Labs supports you from the first conversation through to production use.

Process assessment & opportunity review

We examine a recurring process, such as importing supplier data or preparing a quote. Who's involved, which systems does it use, and where does manual work occur? Together, we check data quality, available interfaces, and exceptions.

You receive a prioritised recommendation covering estimated effort, running costs, and expected benefits. A business-case estimate makes the assumptions explicit. If there isn't a worthwhile starting point, we'll say so.

n8n workflow development & rollout

We design and build workflows with n8n, connect your applications, and test agreed examples and failure cases. The work includes clear responsibility for exceptions, understandable documentation, and handover into operation. We check integration options against the specific systems you use.

AI for documents, enquiries & drafts

AI can prepare unstructured information for the next step: extract details from supplier documents, route enquiries, or draft product descriptions. We integrate these capabilities into your n8n workflows and agree with you how results will be checked and approved.

We select the model based on the task, data requirements, quality, and cost. Locally operated models are an option when they meet the requirements and make operational sense for your business.

Hosting & reliable operation

We help you set up your n8n environment and agree on access, updates, backups, and responsibilities. We explicitly account for data flows, connected services, and the features your project needs. We help you choose, set up, and operate the infrastructure that fits your requirements.

Handover & training

Your team receives a documented introduction to the workflows: what runs automatically, which cases need review, and how changes can be made. We agree on the scope and audience for training upfront. Ongoing support and response times can be arranged separately.

We also offer EUDR employee training, n8n key user training, and administration workshops. Explore training for your team.

How we work together

  1. Free consultation — 30 minutes. Bring one specific process. We'll discuss the problem, the systems involved, and a possible next step. Email us or book a call.
  2. Process assessment — You receive a recommendation on scope, effort, and benefits. We agree on the work and fees before starting.
  3. Pilot & implementation — We test a bounded workflow against agreed criteria, then decide on production use and further steps.
  4. Handover & support — Documentation, training, and clear responsibilities. Further support by agreement.
