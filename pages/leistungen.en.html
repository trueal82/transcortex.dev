Services

Transcortex Labs exists for SMEs that want to automate their processes — without an enterprise budget and without consultant bingo. Project-based collaboration, with a clear scope and a clear outcome.

Potential analysis

Before anything gets built, we look at where your people actually lose time: which processes still run on email, spreadsheets, and paper? Which of them can be automated — and which one gives you the biggest leverage?

The outcome is a prioritized roadmap including an amortization estimate. Honestly meant: if the answer is "nothing here is worth it yet," that is what I will tell you.

n8n workflow development

Design, build, test, and operation of automation workflows with n8n — from connecting your existing systems (email, accounting, CRM, ERP) to production operation. Everything documented and maintainable, so the workflows never turn into a black box only one person understands.

AI/LLM integration

In n8n, language models are not a bolted-on afterthought but a core building block: AI agent nodes (built on LangChain), RAG over your own documents, extraction and classification of unstructured input — invoices, emails, inquiries. I build LLMs into your processes as a work horse, not as a gimmick on the website.

If your data must not leave the building: the whole stack can run self-hosted, language models included.

Self-hosting & digital sovereignty

n8n is open source and runs on your infrastructure — optionally with locally operated language models. Your data stays with you; there is no obligation toward any cloud provider. That is not an ideology debate but an option I can set up for you — know-how from my own server routine, which I document openly on my blog.

Training & enablement

At the end of the project your team should be able to develop the workflows themselves — not be tied to me. Documented handover, onboarding of your people, and afterwards I am reachable exactly when you need me.
