What we agree before starting

  • Which models and features are required; providers cannot all be integrated in the same way.
  • What data is processed, where it flows, and which provider approvals are needed.

A central contract does not replace the review of underlying services. We work with procurement, IT, and data protection stakeholders to establish a suitable arrangement.
