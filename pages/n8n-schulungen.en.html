n8n for key users

For employees who want to understand existing workflows and optionally make small changes themselves. We practise in a test environment using examples from your working day.

  • Read triggers, steps, and data flows; understand executions and error messages.
  • Practise agreed changes to notification text, field mappings, or simple filters.
  • Test with sample data, check the impact, and document changes.
  • Practise approval and rollback; recognise when a change needs support.

Key users can take on agreed small changes. We define the scope and appropriate access rights together.

n8n administration & operations

For internal IT and technical owners. Training follows your hosting model: your own infrastructure or n8n Cloud.

  • Manage users and available roles and permissions for your edition.
  • Organise credentials and connections; monitor execution data and errors.
  • For self-hosting: plan updates, backups, and recovery, and work through them in a suitable training environment.
  • For SaaS: distinguish your responsibilities from those of the platform provider; document ownership and escalation paths.

An operations guide tailored to your environment, with clear responsibilities. We can continue to support more complex changes and incidents.

Work independently within an agreed scope

We document which changes your team can make and when support is useful. We remain available for more complex adjustments. Explore process automation.

How we plan the training

We agree the audience, prior knowledge, and learning goals. You receive a proposal with scope, format, and price. Exercises, materials, time for questions, and a training record form part of the agreed programme. Training is available on its own or alongside implementation.
