When your team fills the gaps between applications

A supplier sends a spreadsheet, product details need updating in your online shop, and a customer enquiry needs information from your ERP. In between, someone copies information, follows up, or enters the same details again. These handovers take time and create opportunities for mistakes.

We support small and medium-sized businesses in Germany, with a particular focus on retail, e-commerce, and manufacturers with complex product information and sales operations. Together, we choose one specific process and assess which steps we can automate reliably. Your existing applications are the starting point.

Connect your systems. Automate with a clear purpose.

Our approach combines enterprise IT, generative AI, and hands-on implementation. It builds on our founder's 15-plus years of professional experience in retail and systems integration. Three principles guide our work:

  • n8n connects your applications: Information moves through suitable interfaces, and recurring steps follow agreed rules.
  • AI supports specific tasks: Such as processing documents, routing enquiries, or drafting text, with defined checks and approvals.
  • Hosting fits your requirements: We agree on hosting, access, and data flows upfront. Where needed, we assess self-hosting and locally operated AI models.
