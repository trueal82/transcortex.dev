About Transcortex Labs

Transcortex Labs builds automation that works in everyday business. We combine consulting, systems integration, and generative AI with hands-on implementation. Our aim is to help SMEs get more from their existing applications and automate recurring business processes step by step.

Founded by Alexander Trümper

Alexander Trümper brings over 15 years of experience in enterprise IT and systems integration. He started in retail, with responsibility for inventory, product ranges, and customer service. Later, he developed and integrated the systems behind those operations. That combination of business understanding and technical delivery shapes Transcortex Labs. The founder currently handles consulting and implementation personally.

Our founder's experience & qualifications

  • Retail & operations: At BAUHAUS and HORNBACH, Alexander worked on connections between POS, ERP, and e-commerce systems, including synchronisation of prices, stock, and product data.
  • Integration & consulting: At valantic, he was technical lead for a commerce migration involving six storefronts and seven ERP systems. At Arineo, he led technical workshops and developed architectures and prototypes.
  • Product data & AI: At Ferrero, his architecture work covers global product data across 13 regions and the development and integration of generative AI applications.
  • Software architecture & integration: iSAQB Certified Professional for Software Architecture (CPSA-F, 2018) and SAP Integration Suite (2023).
  • AI development: Azure AI Engineer Associate and Azure AI Fundamentals (2022). Since 2024, Alexander has been furthering his knowledge through Applied Artificial Intelligence studies.
  • n8n: Course Level 1, complemented by practical work with workflows, interfaces, and his own infrastructure.

That experience informs what we aim to deliver in every project: clear requirements, verifiable results, and a documented handover. Workflows should remain understandable and be able to evolve with your business. For practical work with Python, local AI models, and self-hosted infrastructure, explore Alexander's technical blog.
