Turn requirements into practical tasks

We look at AI applications you are planning or already using. Together, we practise identifying roles, risks, and open questions, then routing them to the right people.

  • Understand the basics: how do your business role, intended use, and potential risks relate?
  • Assess use cases: discuss prohibited practices, potential high-risk applications, and transparency requirements through examples.
  • Clarify responsibilities: who approves use, reviews results, and handles questions or incidents?
  • Build AI literacy: what knowledge do employees need for their tasks, and how can training be documented?

You receive practical exercises, guidance, and a list of questions for your business to address next. We agree the content and regulatory reference date with your subject-matter leads before the session.

What your team takes away

We work on an overview of your AI applications, a responsibilities template, and an internal approval checklist. For example, the workshop explores the questions to resolve before introducing an internal AI assistant. Open points for IT, data protection, and legal advisers are recorded clearly.

Relevant to your roles and applications

Management and project leads need guidance for decisions; business teams and IT need clear working processes. We agree the audience, prior knowledge, and learning goals. You receive a proposal with scope, training lead, format, and price, with remote or on-site sessions by arrangement.

Materials, exercises, time for questions, and a training record form part of the agreed programme. The record documents participation and content; it does not certify your company’s legal compliance.
