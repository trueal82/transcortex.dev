What we practise

  • Describe a task clearly and check the response against sources.
  • Recognise errors, invented details, and inappropriate instructions.
  • Use examples to determine which data is approved for a tool.
  • Apply review requirements, internal policies, and reporting procedures to a concrete case.

A record that makes the training understandable

You receive a training record with the date, duration, content, and learning objectives, together with attendance documentation. A short assessment can be included, giving your internal owners a clear record of what was covered.

This record documents training, not the legal compliance of the organisation. The EU AI Act does not impose a general external certification requirement for AI literacy. We agree appropriate measures for your roles and applications with you and your subject-matter owners.
