# Whitepaper source and publication notes

The German text is based on `transcortex-whitepaper-de-ueberarbeitet.docx`, the corrected edition supplied by the user. The older DE and EN originals were deliberately not used: they contain claims removed during the prior editorial review.

The English text is a new aligned translation of that corrected German edition. This is not represented as the missing final English document mentioned in the earlier task. Both web editions add the same explicitly illustrative business-case calculation and update the contact options. Research findings and their qualifications are preserved; there is no claim of guaranteed ROI or automatic GDPR compliance.

`de.json` and `en.json` are the editable text and bibliography. `scripts/build-whitepapers.py` produces both five-page PDFs. Reference links are preserved within the PDFs, not loaded as website resources. The PNG covers are local renderings of page 1. The older source DOCX files are not altered.

The calculation uses 300 cases/month, 12 minutes before and 5 minutes after (including review), a €40/hour capacity value, €250/month running costs and €9,000 initial implementation. These are hypothetical assumptions, not a quote, client result, study finding, or cash-saving guarantee.
